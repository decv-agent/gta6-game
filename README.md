PALMERA HEAT drops you into Palmera City, a sunlit coastal sprawl reimagined as an original top-down open-world crime game. Wander the blocks on foot or jack any vehicle you spot and take it for a spin. You play through a compact three-mission story built around courier Rico Sol, weaving between moving traffic and crowds of pedestrians while trouble escalates around you. Cause enough chaos and the five-star wanted system kicks in, sending police units after you in proper chases. Gunplay is in the mix too, and everything is tracked on a complete HUD with a live minimap. The whole thing ships as plain static files, so there is no build step to wrestle with.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrow keys** — move Rico around, on foot or behind the wheel
- **Shift** — sprint while on foot
- **E or F** — get into a nearby car, or climb back out
- **Mouse** — aim your weapon; **left click** to fire
- **Esc or P** — pause and resume
- **R** — replay the story from the start
